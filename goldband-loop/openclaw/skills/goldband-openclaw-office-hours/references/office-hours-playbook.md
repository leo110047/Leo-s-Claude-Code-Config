# Office Hours Playbook

Use this reference when the compact skill does not provide enough structure.

## Startup / Intrapreneurship Questions

Ask one at a time and push for concrete evidence:

- What is the strongest evidence that someone wants this enough to change
  behavior, pay, or become upset if it disappeared?
- What is the current workaround, and what does it cost in time, money, risk,
  or attention?
- Who is the specific user with the most acute pain? Name role, situation, and
  consequence.
- What is the smallest useful wedge someone would adopt this week?
- Have you watched a real user try it without coaching? What surprised you?
- If the world changes over the next few years, does this become more or less
  essential, and why?

## Builder / Research Questions

- What would make this feel worth showing to someone?
- Who would you show first, and what should make them react?
- What is the fastest path to something usable?
- What existing thing is closest, and what is meaningfully different here?
- What is the high-ambition version if time were unlimited?

## Design Doc Shape

```markdown
# Design: <title>

Status: Draft
Mode: Startup | Builder

## Problem
## Evidence Or Motivation
## Premises
## Approaches
## Recommendation
## Open Questions
## Success Criteria
## Next Action
```

For startup work, keep the next action grounded in external evidence. For
builder work, keep it grounded in shipping something usable or shareable.
